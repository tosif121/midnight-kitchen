# Fresh Squeeze — Quick Start

1. **Start the local server**:
   ```bash
   python3 -m http.server 8080
   ```

2. **Open in browser**:
   ```
   http://localhost:8080
   ```

3. **Play**:
   - Click **"Turn on my camera"**
   - Grab the straw with your hand
   - Bring it to your lips
   - Pucker your lips to sip fresh cold orange juice!
   - Press **`R`** anytime to refill the glass.
